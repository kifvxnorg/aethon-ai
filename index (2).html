FAQ

Questions
Worth Asking.

Straight answers to the things founders and operators ask us most before working together.

How long does a typical project take?
Simple automations go live in 48–72 hours. Full-stack workflow systems typically take 1–2 weeks. Web redesigns and landing pages run 1–3 weeks depending on scope. We scope everything in the discovery call so you know exactly what to expect before we start.
Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?
Not at all. Our job is to translate your business problem into a technical solution — not the other way around. You tell us what's slowing you down or costing you money. We handle everything from design to deployment.
What happens if something breaks after launch?
Every project includes a post-launch support period. For ongoing retainer clients, we provide continuous monitoring and fixes. Automations are built with error handling and alerting baked in — so we know before you do if something needs attention.
How do you price your projects?
We price per project, not per hour — so you always know what you're paying upfront. Automation projects start from $1,500. Web development from $2,500. Full-stack systems and retainers are custom-scoped. Book a call and we'll give you a clear number within 24 hours.
Can you integrate with the tools we already use?
Yes — we work with 18+ tools including Shopify, HubSpot, Airtable, Notion, Slack, Gmail, Stripe, Twilio, and most CRMs or data platforms. If it has an API, we can connect it. We don't ask you to change your stack.
What's the first step to get started?
Book a free 30-minute strategy call. We'll ask about your current workflow, identify the highest-ROI automation or web opportunity, and give you a clear recommendation — no sales pressure, no commitment required. Most clients leave the call with a clear action plan regardless.
